Why this exists

I wanted the study tool.
I didn’t want the subscription.

LectureLens started from a simple student problem: lecture transcription, summaries, and searchable notes are useful — but the best versions are often locked behind monthly plans. The product goal is to build the useful core myself, keep the source evidence visible, and make the system technically honest.

Working product

Your lecture, turned into a searchable source.

Record audio in-browser or load the demo. LectureLens stores sessions locally, segments transcript text, builds semantic embeddings, and returns the strongest source passages for a question.

Privacy by architecture

Your lecture should not need a cloud account just to become useful.

LectureLens is designed around browser-side recording, local storage, and client-side retrieval. The current build does not require a LectureLens backend for transcript indexing or semantic search.
